steps:
- text: Heat a large Dutch oven or heavy-bottomed pot over medium-high heat and add the oil. Add the ground
    pork and season with salt, pepper, ½ tsp curry powder, and ½ tsp cumin. Let the pork cook undisturbed
    for 4–5 minutes to develop a deep golden crust, then break it up and continue cooking until fully
    browned and lightly caramelized.
  timer: 300
- text: Add a small splash of fish sauce and soy sauce to the pork if desired, then remove it with a slotted
    spoon and set aside.
- text: Lower the heat to medium/medium-low. Add a touch more oil if needed, then add the shallot, lemongrass,
    grated ginger, grated garlic, and the white parts of the green onions. Cook for 1–2 minutes until
    very fragrant.
  timer: 120
- text: Stir in the red curry and tomato paste and cook for 2–3 minutes, letting it caramelize slightly
    and deepen in color.
  timer: 180
- text: Add the curry powder, cumin, coriander, turmeric, and brown sugar. Stir constantly for about 30
    seconds to bloom the spices.
  timer: 30
- text: 'Optional but recommended: deglaze the pot with a few splashes of fish sauce and/or soy sauce,
    scraping up any browned bits from the bottom.'
- text: Pour in both cans of coconut milk and stir well to fully dissolve the curry paste and spices.
- text: Add the chicken broth, fish sauce, and soy sauce. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook uncovered
    for 10–15 minutes to let the flavors meld. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed.
  timer: 900
- text: While the broth simmers, cook the noodles separately according to package directions. Drain and
    toss lightly with a touch of sesame oil so they don’t stick.
- text: Add the cooked pork back into the broth and simmer for 3–5 minutes so it absorbs the flavor.
  timer: 300
- text: Add the bok choy directly to the broth, cover, and steam gently for 2–3 minutes just until tender
    and bright green. (For filming or extra-pretty bowls, you can blanch it separately instead.)
  timer: 180
- text: Remove and discard the lemongrass. Finish the soup with lime zest, lime juice, and a small drizzle
    of toasted sesame oil. Taste one last time and adjust seasoning.
- text: To serve, add noodles to bowls and ladle the hot broth, pork, and bok choy over the top.
- text: Finish with Thai chili slices, chili oil or chili crisp, cilantro or Thai basil, green onion tops,
    and extra lime. Serve hot.
