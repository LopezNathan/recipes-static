steps:
- text: Preheat oven to 400 degrees F.
- text: Heat olive oil in a large oven-proof skillet over medium high heat. Add ground beef, garlic and
    onion to the skillet, and cook until beef has browned, about 3-5 minutes, making sure to crumble the
    beef as it cooks; season with salt and pepper, to taste. Drain excess fat.
  timer: 300
- text: Stir in pasta sauce, chicken broth, red pepper flakes and spaghetti; season with salt and pepper,
    to taste.
- text: Bring to a boil; reduce heat and simmer, stirring occasionally, until pasta is cooked through,
    about 18-20 minutes.
  timer: 1200
- text: Create 4 small wells with the back of a spoon. Gently crack eggs and slide it into each well,
    keeping the yolk intact. Sprinkle with cheeses; season with salt and pepper, to taste.
- text: Place into oven and bake until the egg whites have set but the yolks are still runny, about 7-9
    minutes.
  timer: 540
- text: Serve immediately, garnished with parsley, if desired.
