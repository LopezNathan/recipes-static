steps:
- text: Juice the orange, lemons and limes. Add juice to blender or food processor. Keep one half of the
    juiced orange, lemon and lime rind.
- text: Add cilantro, garlic, onion, soy sauce and olive oil to juice. Blend or pulse together until onion
    and cilantro are well minced.
- text: Add cumin, paprika, salt and pepper. Mix for a few seconds in blender or food processor.
- text: In a plastic freezer bag, add chicken breasts and orange, lemon and lime rind. You may have to
    cut rinds into wedges to fit.
- text: Next add marinade to chicken and rinds. Seal bag tightly and massage marinade into chicken. Refrigerate
    4 hours or overnight is best.
  timer: 14400
- text: Heat grill to medium high heat. (375 to 450 degrees)
- text: Grill chicken 4-6 minutes on one side; then flip and grill other side 4-5 minutes or until internal
    temperature reaches 160 degrees. Let rest 5 minutes before serving.
