steps:
- text: Before you get started cooking, you’ll need to prep your chicken breasts. To do so, slice the
    chicken breasts in half horizontally to get two thin pieces. Place one thin slice of chicken breast
    between two sheets of plastic wrap and pound with a meat tenderizer until the chicken is ¼” thick.
    Season on both sides with salt and pepper, and then repeat with the rest of the chicken.
- text: Next, press the chicken breasts into the flour and shake off the excess so they are evenly coated.
    Heat the oil in a large skillet and cook the chicken breasts in batches until golden brown, about
    two minutes on each side. Remove the chicken and rest on a plate.
- text: Use the same skillet so that you get all the browned bits from the chicken in your sauce. Add
    the chicken broth, lemon juice, and wine to the pan and bring to a boil. Stir occasionally, but let
    the liquid cook continuously for about 5 minutes until it’s reduced by about a quarter. Now, lower
    the heat. It’s time to add the butter! Add the butter one cube at a time while whisking continuously.
    Make sure not to add the butter too fast, wait until each cube melts before adding the next. If the
    sauce begins to break, add a tablespoon of water to the sauce and whisk until the sauce is smooth
    and glossy again.
  timer: 300
- text: Add the chicken and capers back to the skillet. Bring the sauce back to a low simmer and cook
    just until the chicken is warmed through. The sauce should be thick enough to coat the back of your
    spoon. Serve with extra lemon wedges alongside!
- text: Cut chicken breasts in half parallel to work surface all the way through. In between 2 sheets
    of plastic wrap, pound each breast until 1/4" thick; generously season with salt and pepper.
- text: In a large shallow bowl, place flour. Dredge chicken in flour, shaking off excess. In a large
    skillet over high heat, heat 1/4 cup oil until very hot. Working with 3 chicken pieces at a time,
    cook, turning once, until golden brown, about 2 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate. Adjust heat
    as needed and repeat with remaining chicken.
  timer: 120
- text: In same skillet over high heat, bring wine, broth, and lemon juice to a boil. Cook, stirring occasionally,
    until thickened and reduced by about one-quarter, about 5 minutes. Reduce heat to low and add butter,
    one piece at a time, vigorously whisking after each addition; don’t stop whisking until all butter
    is incorporated. If sauce starts to break, add 1 tablespoon water and continue to whisk until sauce
    is smooth and glossy.
  timer: 300
- text: Return chicken to skillet. Add capers, bring sauce to a light simmer, and cook until warmed through,
    about 3 minutes. Serve with lemon wedges alongside.
  timer: 180
