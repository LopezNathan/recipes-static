Prevent Scorching: Once you add the milk, stir continuously so that lumps don't form and the pot doesn't scorch on the bottom. The bottom edges of the pan often scorch more easily, so be sure to get your whisk in there!Making a Roux: Don't let your butter + flour brown too much. You want the mixture to be a golden color before you add the milk. This will create a light, creamy sauce.Adjusting the Consistency: If the sauce is too thick, add a little milk to thin out the sauce.
