steps:
- text: Combine the ground beef, 1 cup of cheese, bread crumbs, parsley, garlic, jalapeño, egg, and cumin in a medium bowl. Season with salt and pepper, mix until combined, and form into meatballs.
- text: Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the meatballs in a single layer and sear for 2 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate.
  timer: 240
- text: Add the onion to the skillet and cook, stirring, until soft, about 5 minutes. Stir in the crushed tomatoes and chipotle in adobo and bring to a boil.
  timer: 300
- text: Reduce the heat to medium-low and return the meatballs to the skillet. Cover and simmer until cooked through, about 10 minutes.
  timer: 600
- text: Top with the remaining 1 cup cheese, cover, and let the cheese melt for about 2 minutes.
  timer: 120
- text: Garnish with parsley and serve.
