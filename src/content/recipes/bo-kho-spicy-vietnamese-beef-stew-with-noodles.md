steps:
- text: Combine the beef with the garlic, ginger, fish sauce, five-spice powder, and brown sugar until evenly coated. Marinate for 30 minutes.
  timer: 1800
- text: Heat the oil in a large stockpot or Dutch oven over high heat. Add the lemongrass lengths and infuse the oil for 1 minute. Add the minced lemongrass and garlic and cook for 2 minutes.
  timer: 120
- text: Add the onion and cook until translucent. Add the marinated beef and brown evenly on all sides. Add the tomato paste and cook uncovered for 5 minutes.
  timer: 300
- text: Add the water, coconut water, star anise, black pepper, chili powder, annatto, and paprika. Bring to a boil, reduce the heat to medium-low, and simmer covered for 1 hour.
  timer: 3600
- text: Add the carrots, salt, soy sauce, and chili oil. Simmer for another 40 minutes.
  timer: 2400
- text: Remove the large lemongrass stalks and any star anise pods. Cook the noodles according to the package instructions, transfer to bowls, and ladle the stew over the top.
- text: Garnish with cilantro, Thai basil, and raw onion. Serve with lime wedges.
