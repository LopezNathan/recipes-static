---
title: Creamy Cajun Shrimp Pasta
servings: 4
time:
  prep: 15
  cook: 45
tags:
- dinner
- creamy
- pasta
source: https://www.delish.com/cooking/recipe-ideas/a64320789/creamy-cajun-pasta-recipe/
ingredients:
- qty: 1
  unit: lb
  item: large shrimp, peeled, deveined, tails removed, patted dry
- qty: 2
  unit: tsp
  item: store-bought or homemade Cajun seasoning
- qty: 5
  unit: tsp
  item: extra-virgin olive oil, divided
- qty: 1
  unit: null
  item: yellow onion, coarsely chopped
- qty: 1
  unit: null
  item: red bell pepper, seeds and ribs removed, coarsely chopped
- qty: 4
  unit: null
  item: garlic cloves
- qty: 12
  unit: oz
  item: linguine
- qty: 1
  unit: cup
  item: heavy cream
- qty: 3
  unit: oz
  item: Parmesan, finely grated (about 1 1/2 cups), plus more for serving
- qty: 0.5
  unit: cup
  item: finely chopped fresh parsley
- qty: 0.5
  unit: null
  item: thinly sliced chives
steps:
- text: In a large bowl, toss shrimp, Cajun seasoning, and 2 Tbsp. oil; lightly season with salt.
- text: >-
    In a large, deep, 12" skillet over medium-high heat, heat 2 Tbsp. oil. Working in batches, cook shrimp, making sure
    shrimp is touching skillet, until browned and cooked through, about 1 minute per side. Transfer to a plate. Wipe out
    skillet.
- text: >-
    In a food processor, pulse onion, bell pepper, and garlic, scraping down sides if needed, until very smooth and a
    puree forms, about 1 minute.
- text: >-
    In a large pot of boiling salted water, cook pasta, stirring occasionally, until al dente according to package
    directions. Reserve 1 cup pasta water, then drain.
- text: >-
    Meanwhile, in same skillet over medium heat, heat remaining 2 Tbsp. oil. Cook onion mixture, stirring frequently and
    adding a splash of water if browning too quickly, until most of the liquid is evaporated and mixture becomes drier
    and thicker, 7 to 10 minutes.
- text: >-
    Add cream to onion mixture, scraping any bits stuck to bottom of pan. Bring to a boil, then remove from heat. Whisk
    Parmesan into sauce until thick and homogenous. Add shrimp, pasta, parsley, chives, and 3/4 cup pasta water,
    stirring and adding more pasta water if needed, until sauce is smooth and glossy. Top with more Parmesan.
