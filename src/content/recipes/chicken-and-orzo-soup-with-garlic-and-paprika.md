steps:
- text: In a large saucepan, combine the oil, tomato paste and garlic.
- text: Cook, stirring, until several shades darker.
- text: Add the paprika and mint, then cook, stirring, just until fragrant.
- text: Add the chicken, 7 cups water and 2½ teaspoons salt.
- text: Simmer, uncovered, until a skewer inserted into the chicken meets no resistance.
- text: Transfer the chicken to a bowl.
- text: Add the orzo to the pot and cook until al dente.
- text: Shred the chicken.
- text: When the orzo is done, add the chicken and season with salt and pepper.
- text: Serve drizzled with additional oil.
