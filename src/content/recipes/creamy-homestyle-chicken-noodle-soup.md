steps:
- text: Melt the butter In a large Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the onions, garlic, celery, and carrots.
    Season with salt and pepper. Cook for 5 minutes. Stir in the flour, thyme, and sage. Cook another
    5 minutes.
  timer: 300
- text: Pour in the broth and bring to a boil over high heat. Stir in the chicken, bay leaf, and if you
    have one – a parmesan rind. Cover and cook for 20 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through. At
    this point, the soup can also simmer on low for up to 4-6 hours.
  timer: 1200
- text: Shred the chicken, remove the bay leaf. taste, and season with salt and pepper. Bring the soup
    back to a boil, then add the noodles and cook for 6-8 minutes, until soft. Stir in the milk, parmesan,
    and parsley.
  timer: 480
- text: Serve the soup topped with additional parmesan, if desired.
